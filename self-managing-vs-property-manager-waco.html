Frequently asked questions

01

Is a property manager worth it in Waco?

A property manager can be worth it when professional pricing, faster leasing, consistent screening, maintenance coordination, documentation, and compliance reduce vacancy, mistakes, or owner time by more than the management cost.

02

What percentage do property managers charge in Texas?

Texas property management fees vary by company, property, and service package. Compare the monthly fee together with leasing, renewal, maintenance-coordination, inspection, and termination charges.
