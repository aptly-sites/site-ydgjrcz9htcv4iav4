Rental qualifications

Know what to prepare before you apply.

Requirements can vary by home or assistance program. The current written application criteria and final lease terms control.

01

Every adult applies

Each prospective leaseholder age 18 or older completes an application.

02

Document your income

Gross income generally equals three times the rent, supported by recent pay stubs or a tax return.

03

Complete screening

Screening reviews credit, rental and eviction history, financial history, and criminal history. A guarantor may be required.

Common questions

Before you choose a home.

Can you hold a place before I apply?

A home is held only after an application is complete and approved and the security deposit has been paid. Delayed move-in holds are limited to 14 days, and prorated holding days may be deducted if an applicant does not move forward.

Do I pay first and last month’s rent with the security deposit?

No. J R Grace Realty does not collect the last month’s rent, and security deposits are not used for rent payments.

How old must an applicant be?

Applicants must be at least 18 years old to apply for a lease.

Is the security deposit refundable?

Yes. Security deposits are refundable subject to the lease, property condition, outstanding charges, and applicable law.

Who may need a cosigner?

An applicant who cannot qualify through the standard credit, financial, history, or rental-history review may need a guarantor who can qualify.

How do I apply for a property?

Open the home in Rental Search and use its Apply link to continue through the secure application portal.

Current resident questions

Quick answers for life in your home.

How do I pay rent?

Residents can pay online by bank account or credit card, or use approved cash or check payment locations such as Walmart or H-E-B.

What should I do before getting a pet?

Request approval in writing. Once approved, complete the required pet addendum, deposits or fees, and screening requirements before bringing the pet to the property.

Who pays for repairs?

The responsible party depends on the cause. Resident-caused damage is charged to the resident; owner-responsible building or age-related repairs are charged to the owner.

How do I report a maintenance issue?

Submit a request through Resident Web Access or call the office. Include the affected area, what you observed, and any steps already taken to prevent further damage.

What happens after hours?

Call the main office number after hours to reach the maintenance hotline. The team will assess the situation and determine the appropriate response.
