# J R Grace Realty content roadmap

This roadmap translates the September 30, 2026 working positioning guide into new website pages. The priorities reflect how strongly each page can differentiate J R Grace, answer a real owner or resident question, and support search discovery.

## Priority 1: Our Management Process

**Suggested URL:** `/our-management-process.html`

Show the first 30–100 days from initial conversation through a stabilized tenancy. This is the clearest way to prove that J R Grace operates with a disciplined system while still beginning with the owner’s situation.

Recommended sections:

- The fit conversation: goals, concerns, timing, and rent-versus-sell context
- Digital agreement and professional onboarding
- Property inspection, documentation, access, and make-ready recommendations
- Professional photography and market launch
- Weekly leasing updates and strategy recommendations
- Approved resident, move-in, and the transition to steady-state management
- What the owner needs to provide at each stage

Confirm before publishing: the responsible role at each step, typical timing ranges, inspection scope, and the exact communication cadence.

## Priority 2: Owner Benefit Package

**Suggested URL:** `/owner-benefit-package.html`

Explain how the package reduces nickel-and-diming and clarifies what management includes. The page should focus on owner outcomes and plain-English examples rather than presenting a feature inventory without context.

Recommended sections:

- Why the package exists
- Included leasing and renewal support
- Property inspections and HVAC filter support
- Professional photography, floor plans, and video walkthroughs
- Owner reporting, 1099 support, home-warranty coordination, insurance referrals, and HOA communication
- Animal protection and eviction-related provisions
- A transparent “included, at cost, or owner-approved” comparison

Confirm before publishing: current pricing, the final included-benefit list, limits and exclusions, terminology, and any state or contract language.

## Priority 3: What Happens When Something Goes Wrong

**Suggested URL:** `/our-accountability-promise.html`

This page can turn the straight-shooting and OOPS philosophy into a memorable trust differentiator. It should acknowledge that rental ownership is not problem-free and explain how J R Grace responds when an issue is difficult or the company makes a mistake.

Recommended sections:

- Problems no manager can promise to prevent
- How the team communicates what is known and still uncertain
- How recommendations and owner approvals are documented
- The OOPS philosophy: make it right, learn, and improve the system
- Examples of accounting, billing, repair, or workflow issues without identifying clients
- Emergency escalation and after-hours expectations

Confirm before publishing: approved examples, financial limits or internal approval rules, emergency definitions, and response-time language.

## Priority 4: Technology With a Human Option

**Suggested URL:** `/technology-and-service.html`

Describe each digital convenience in terms of the problem it solves, then show how a person remains available. This page would separate J R Grace from both old-fashioned managers and impersonal, portal-only operators.

Recommended sections:

- Owner lead capture and rental analysis
- Digital agreements, applications, document collection, payments, and reporting
- Touchless move-in
- Tracked maintenance and communication workflows
- Shared inbox and team response model
- When phone, email, video, or an in-person meeting is the better option

Confirm before publishing: current software capabilities, support channels, staffed hours, and which in-person options are consistently available.

## Priority 5: Maintenance and Resident Care

**Suggested URL:** `/maintenance-and-resident-care.html`

Connect resident responsiveness to owner asset care. The page should explain intake, triage, in-house maintenance capability, vendor coordination, communication, documentation, and HVAC support without guaranteeing repair times.

Recommended sections:

- How residents report maintenance
- What happens after a request is submitted
- In-house technician work versus outside specialists
- Owner communication and authorization
- After-hours and emergency handling
- Resident Benefit Package support, including temporary AC where applicable
- How recurring issues inform property recommendations

Confirm before publishing: service boundaries, emergency routing, authorization thresholds, temporary-AC eligibility, and any resident charges.

## Priority 6: Property Management First

**Suggested URL:** `/property-management-first.html`

Create a concise comparison page explaining why a management-first company operates differently from a sales-first brokerage that also manages rentals. Keep the tone factual and avoid criticizing named competitors.

Recommended sections:

- Where the team invests its training and systems
- Why ongoing management requires a different operating rhythm from sales
- Compliance, resident experience, maintenance, and owner reporting
- When J R Grace can still help evaluate a sale
- Questions owners should ask any property manager

Confirm before publishing: sales capabilities, referral process, and how rent-versus-sell consultations are handled.

## Priority 7: Training and Professional Standards

**Suggested URL:** `/professional-standards.html`

Make the elite-team concept concrete through training, designations, education, and continuous improvement. “Elite” should describe preparation and standards rather than superiority.

Recommended sections:

- NARPM involvement
- TRLS and TRPM designations
- Fair Housing education
- EPA and lead-based-paint training
- How procedures are documented and improved
- How training affects real owner and resident experiences

Confirm before publishing: current memberships, designation holders, certification status, training frequency, and which credentials apply to which team members.

## Later proof-driven pages

These pages will become valuable after J R Grace gathers approved evidence:

- **Owner case studies:** vacancy, make-ready, leasing, maintenance, or portfolio-transition stories with verified numbers and permission
- **Resident service stories:** examples of responsiveness and maintenance resolution with resident consent
- **Owner reporting guide:** sample statement walkthroughs, reporting schedule, reserve treatment, and year-end documents
- **Emergency response guide:** clear definitions and expectations for owners and residents
- **Team and culture:** roles, training rhythm, shared-inbox model, and how the team collaborates

Avoid publishing claims about guaranteed rent, perfect residents, zero vacancy, fixed repair times, or investment returns. Package pricing, benefit inclusions, response times, and current credentials should be verified immediately before launch.
