07Questions people ask us

Will my email stop working?

No. Your website and your email are separate settings on the domain. We change only the ones that control the website and we confirm your email records are untouched before and after.

Will I lose my Google rankings?

This is the real risk in any site move, and it is why we forward every old page address to its new one. Rankings can wobble for a week or two while Google recrawls, then settle. We watch it daily and tell you what we see.

Will the site be down during the switch?

There is no gap. Visitors see either the old site or the new one during the changeover, which usually lasts minutes.

Can I change things after it is live?

Yes. Content updates are part of your plan. Send them over and we make them.

Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. The domain stays in your name and your account, and the content is yours.

What if I spot something after launch?

Tell us and we fix it. The first two weeks after a launch are when small things surface, and we plan for that.

Questions on any of this, just reply to the email or call. We would rather answer the same question twice than switch your domain over before you are comfortable.
